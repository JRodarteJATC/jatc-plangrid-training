# 3A PlanGrid Project

**Class:** Week 3A – Advanced Specs, Blueprints & Details · **Instructor:** Juan Rodarte
**Turn in:** Settings → **Export backup (.json)** → send the file to your instructor.
Your work is graded automatically from that file (100 points).

You can do the project in the sample project (*Central Valley Training Center – Bldg B*) **or** in
your own project with real plans:
**Settings → New project**, then **Sheets → Upload** your plan PDF. Juan Rodarte is already on the team
of every project. If you have more than one project, the one with the best work is graded.

> Use the **same device and browser** every day and export a backup at the end of each class.

---

## 1. Five days of Daily Reports – 45 pts
**Daily Reports & Time Sheets → + Daily Report.** Make one for **5 different days** (change the date).

| Section | What to put |
|---|---|
| Manpower | Trade/classification, company, number of workers, hours |
| **Work log** | What was installed today, where (area, grid lines), how much (squares, LF) |
| **Material log** | One row per material: material, quantity, unit, supplier/ticket, where used. **+ Row** for more |
| **Equipment log** | One row per piece of equipment: name, qty, hours used, notes |
| **Notes** | Anything else about the work that day – weather effects, coordination, problems, what's next |
| **Photos** | **+ Add photo** – in at least **3 of the 5** reports |

Click **Submit** (not only *Save draft*).

## 2. Documents for the materials – 10 pts
Find the manufacturer's **product data sheets, spec sheets, brochures, SDS or installation guides** for the
materials in your material log (search the internet). **Documents & Specs → Upload file**, folder
**Materials**. Upload at least **2**. (PDF or image, 8 MB max each.)

## 3. Time Sheet for each day – 15 pts
**Daily Reports & Time Sheets → + Time Sheet**, **same date** as each daily report.
One row per worker: name, classification, start, end, lunch (minutes) – hours fill in automatically –
and cost code/task. Click **Submit**.

## 4. Three Tasks – 20 pts
Pin them on the plans: open a sheet, pick the **Issue** tool (`I`), click the spot. Then:

- **Type: Task**
- **Assigned to:** a team member
- **Watching:** check **Juan Rodarte**
- Detailed **description** (what, where, why, what to do)
- **Photo(s)** for reference – **+ Add photo**
- **Delay (days)** and **Cost increase ($)** – enter **0** if there is none
- **Markups on the plan** for the task (cloud, arrow, text, dimension…) → **Publish** them
  (select a markup → **Publish**, or **Publish all** in the side panel)

## 5. Three RFIs to the instructor – 20 pts
**RFIs → + New RFI.** Ask *real* questions a roofer would have on this job (conflicts between plans, details
and specs, missing information, field conditions).

- **Assigned to:** Juan Rodarte
- **Response due** date
- Reference the **sheets**, and mark up those sheets → **Publish** the markups
- Click **Save & Send** – the **Sent date** is filled in automatically
- **Getting the answer:** turn in your backup (Settings → Export backup). When your instructor sends back the
  **RFI answers file**, open **RFIs → ⤒ Import instructor answers** and choose it. Your RFIs change to
  **Answered** and show the official answer. Close them once the answer is shared with the crew.
