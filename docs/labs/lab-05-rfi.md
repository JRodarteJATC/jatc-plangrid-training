# Lab 5 – Find the Conflict, Write the RFI

> **Answer in the app:** Quizzes & Worksheets → *Lab 5 – RTU-4 Conflict Worksheet*. The app version is what gets graded (automatically). Your work in the app (markups, RFIs, reports) is auto-graded too – turn in your backup file.

**Time:** 40 min · **Module:** 5 · **Turn in:** printed RFI (🖨 Print on the RFI) + backup file

## Scenario
ASI-01 added **RTU-4** to the roof. Before the mechanical sub sets the curb, your foreman wants you
to check that the roofing will work.

## Tasks
1. Open **Documents → ASIs & Bulletins → ASI-01**. How tall is the RTU-4 curb? What sheets did the
   ASI revise? ______
2. Open **R-101 (Rev 1)**. Where is RTU-4 (how far from the low line at y = 32')? ______
3. Open **R-102 Tapered Insulation Plan**. How thick is the insulation at the RTU-4 curb (find the
   thickness lines on each side of it)? Remember to add the **1/2" cover board** from R-601. ______
4. Detail **4/R-501** requires how much curb flashing above the roof? ______
5. Do the math: curb height – total roof thickness at the curb = flashing height available. Is it
   enough? ______
6. Is the RTU-4 cricket shown on R-102? ______ What did the architect say about the tapered layout
   submittal? ______
7. Write **one** RFI (pick the most important question – curb height *or* cricket):
   - Subject (one line, specific)
   - Question with references (ASI-01, R-101 Rev 1, R-102, 4/R-501, R-601)
   - Suggested solution
   - Cost / schedule impact
   - Referenced sheets and assigned to Priya Shah (architect)
8. **Save & Send**.
9. Swap with a partner. Play the architect: answer their RFI and save it.
10. Close your RFI once answered and put a **SEE RFI** stamp at RTU-4 on R-101.
11. Log a submittal for **07 72 00 – Roof Accessories – RTU-4 curb** (Shop Drawings).

## RFI grading rubric (/10)
| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject | missing/vague | general | specific, one line |
| Question | unclear | clear, no references | clear + sheets + detail + ASI |
| Suggested solution | none | vague | specific and buildable |
| Impacts | none | one | cost and schedule both addressed |
| Professional, one question only | no | partly | yes |
