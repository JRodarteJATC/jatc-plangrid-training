## RFIs – Request for Information
An RFI is a **formal written question** to the architect (or roof consultant) when the documents
are unclear, conflict, or won't work in the field. It creates a dated record, and the answer can
change cost or schedule.

The foreman or PM usually sends RFIs – but **you** will often be the one who finds the problem:
a parapet that's too low, a penetration too close to a curb, a drain at the high point.

### Workflow
```
Draft ──send──▶ Open (ball in court: architect) ──answer──▶ Answered ──distribute──▶ Closed
```

### How to write a good RFI
1. **Subject:** one line. `North parapet too low for 8" base flashing`
2. **Question:** state the conflict with references – sheet, detail, spec section, location.
3. **Suggested solution:** make it easy to say yes. `Add 2x6 treated nailer and raise coping.`
4. **Impact:** cost? schedule? warranty? Unknown is OK.
5. **Attach** a marked-up snip or photo with a tape measure in it.
6. **One question per RFI.**

Bad: *"Flashing doesn't fit. What do we do?"*
Good: see **RFI-001** in the app – it names the detail (1/R-501), the insulation thickness (R-102),
the measured height, and proposes a fix.

### Step by step
1. Read **RFI-001** in the app (already answered).
2. **RFIs → + New RFI.** Write the RTU-4 question from Lab 5.
3. Reference **R-101** (and R-102), assign **Priya Shah** (architect), due in 7 days.
4. **Save & Send** → **Open**.
5. Play the architect: open it, type an **Official answer**, **Save** → **Answered**.
6. **Close RFI**, then stamp **SEE RFI** on the sheet where it applies.

## Submittals
Before material is bought, the contractor submits **product data, shop drawings, samples and the
manufacturer's warranty letter** for approval. Organized by **CSI MasterFormat**. Roofing and
waterproofing live in **Division 07 – Thermal & Moisture Protection**:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 07 13 26 | Self-adhering sheet waterproofing |
| 07 22 00 | Roof & deck insulation (tapered layout) |
| 07 52 00 | Modified bitumen membrane roofing |
| 07 54 23 | TPO roofing |
| 07 62 00 | Sheet metal flashing & trim (coping, counterflashing) |
| 07 72 00 | Roof accessories (hatches, curbs, walkway pads) |
| 07 92 00 | Joint sealants |

| Status | Meaning for the crew |
|---|---|
| Approved | Order and install |
| Approved as Noted | Order, but follow the reviewer's notes |
| Revise & Resubmit | **Don't order.** Fix and resubmit |
| Rejected | Don't order. Different product needed |

**Field rule:** never install a membrane, insulation or adhesive that isn't on the approved
submittal – substitutions can **void the manufacturer's warranty**.
