# Module 8 – Moving to the Real App

**Time:** 20 min

You've practiced every core workflow. Here's how to make the jump to the software your contractor
actually uses.

## Which app will I see?
- **PlanGrid** – long-time field favorite. Autodesk has been moving PlanGrid customers to
  **Autodesk Build** (Autodesk Construction Cloud). Many jobs still say "PlanGrid" for either one.
- **Procore** – very common with general contractors.
- **Fieldwire** – popular with specialty contractors.
- **Bluebeam Revu** – PDF markup and **roofing takeoff**; estimators live in it.
- **Manufacturer apps / portals** – many membrane manufacturers have their own inspection and
  warranty apps. The same skills apply.

Use the [feature map](../feature-map.md) to find each tool.

## First day checklist
1. Get invited to the project by your foreman/PM (you'll get an email).
2. Install the mobile app, sign in, open the project.
3. **Download sheets for offline use** – roofs often have poor signal.
4. Find the **current version set**. Confirm with your foreman which set to build from.
5. Find the **roof plan, tapered layout, details and roofing specs**. Tag/favorite them.
6. Ask for your company's **markup colors** and **issue types**.
7. Ask what you're allowed to **publish**.

## Tablets on a roof
- Use a case with a hand strap and a lanyard – a dropped tablet is a falling object.
- Glare: turn brightness up, use a matte screen protector.
- Heat: don't leave the tablet on black membrane or in the sun.
- Keep your fall protection on – never walk and read at the same time near an edge.

## Etiquette
- Everything you publish is permanent, time-stamped and has your name on it.
- Owners, architects and manufacturer reps may see issues and photos – keep it professional.
- Don't photograph people without permission.
- Sync regularly.
