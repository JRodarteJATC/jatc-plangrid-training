# Module 2 – Markups & Layers

**Time:** 45 min · **App missions:** Module 2 (6 missions) · **Lab:** [Lab 2 – Mark It Up](../labs/lab-02-markups.md)

## Objectives
- Use the pen, highlighter, shapes, revision cloud, arrow, text and stamp tools.
- Choose colors with meaning and keep markups readable.
- Understand **personal**, **published** and **as-built** layers.
- Select, move, recolor, publish and delete markups; undo mistakes.
- Create a hyperlink to another sheet.

## Why it matters on the roof
Markups are how the crew talks on the drawings: "ponding here," "boot moved 2 ft," "seam
re-welded," "Area A dried-in to this line." A clean markup saves a trip up the ladder; a messy one
causes a leak. At closeout your **as-built markups** show the owner where every drain, curb and
penetration really is – and the manufacturer may need them for the warranty.

## The tools
| Tool | Key | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| ☝ Select / Pan | `V` | Move around, pick a markup, click hyperlinks and pins |
| ✎ Pen | `P` | Quick freehand notes |
| ▰ Highlighter | `H` | Show progress – e.g. highlight the area dried-in today |
| ╱ Line / ➚ Arrow | `L` / `A` | Point at things, show a flashing run |
| ▭ Rectangle / ◯ Ellipse | `R` / `E` | Box or circle an area |
| ☁ Revision cloud | `C` | Show something that changed or needs attention |
| T Text | `T` | Write the note – always explain *why* |
| ⬒ Stamp | `S` | FIELD VERIFY, AS-BUILT, LEAK, PROBED OK, HOLD, SEE RFI… |
| 🔗 Hyperlink | `Y` | Link a note to another sheet or a web page (e.g. mfr detail) |

**Undo** `Ctrl+Z` · **Redo** `Ctrl+Y` · **Delete selected** `Del`

## Layers – who can see it?
| Layer | Who sees it | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | Only you | Your own notes, practice, takeoffs |
| **Published** | Whole project team | Anything the foreman / GC / architect / mfr rep needs to see |
| **As-Built** (dashed) | Team, turned over at closeout | What was *actually* installed when it differs from the plans |

In PlanGrid / Autodesk Build markups are personal until you **publish** them. Published markups
are part of the job record – think before you publish.

## Color conventions (common – check your company's standard)
- **Red** – problems, leaks, deletions
- **Blue / green** – completed work, as-builts
- **Yellow highlighter** – area dried-in / completed today
- **Black** – general notes

## Step by step
1. Open **R-101 Roof Plan**.
2. Press `C` and drag a cloud around the ponding area NE of **RD-2**.
3. Press `T`, click next to it and type: `Ponding 1/2" – check sump & cricket before membrane`.
4. Press `V`, click your cloud. The side panel shows who made it and when. Change its color.
5. Click **Publish**. The badge changes from *personal* to *published*.
6. Press `S`, pick **FIELD VERIFY**, click near **RTU-3**.
7. Pick any tool, set **Layer = As-Built** and draw an arrow showing a pipe boot "moved 2' west."
   As-built lines are dashed.
8. Highlight (yellow) the part of Roof Area A you "dried-in today."
9. Press `Y`, drag a box around Roof Note 1 and link it to **R-102**.
10. In the side panel, uncheck **Personal** – personal markups disappear. Recheck it.
11. `Ctrl+Z` a few times, then `Ctrl+Y` to bring them back.
12. Click **⤓ PNG** to export the sheet with your markups – handy for texting the foreman.

## Good markup habits
- One idea per markup; always add a **text note** that says what and why.
- Don't cover up drain tags, dimensions or detail bubbles.
- Use **personal** for scratch work. Clean up before publishing.
