# Module 1 – Getting Started & Navigating Sheets

**Time:** 45 min · **App missions:** Module 1 (5 missions) · **Lab:** [Lab 1 – Scavenger Hunt](../labs/lab-01-scavenger-hunt.md)

## Objectives
By the end of this module you can:
- Explain why contractors use plan-management apps instead of paper sets.
- Open a project, find a sheet by number or title, and move between sheets.
- Zoom, pan and fit a sheet on a computer and on a tablet.
- Follow a detail callout hyperlink and get back.
- Find a spec section and search inside it.

## Why it matters on the roof
A roofing crew can't carry a 36"×24" paper set up a ladder in the wind. On a tablet in
PlanGrid / Autodesk Build, every roofer sees the **same current set**. When the architect adds a
rooftop unit or moves a drain, the office publishes the new sheet once and everyone's tablet
updates. Building off an old roof plan means tearing off and redoing work you already warrantied.

You'll use the app to:
- Find **where** your work is (roof area, drain, curb, wall) and **how** it's built (the detail).
- Check **thicknesses, slopes, flashing heights** and **laps** before you start.
- Record what you find (markups, photos, issues) where the whole team can see it.

## Key terms
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Project** | One job. Everything (sheets, issues, RFIs) lives inside it. |
| **Sheet** | One drawing page, identified by its **sheet number** (R-101) and title (Roof Plan). |
| **Discipline** | The letter prefix: A = Architectural, R = Roofing (some jobs put roofing on A-sheets), W = Waterproofing, S = Structural, M = Mechanical. |
| **Version set** | A group of sheets issued together (Bid Set, Permit Set, ASI-01, Bulletin 3). |
| **Current version** | The newest version of a sheet. Always build from the current version. |
| **Title block** | The box on the right with project name, sheet number, revisions and date. |
| **Callout / detail bubble** | A circle split in half: top = detail number, bottom = sheet it's on (2 / R-501). |

## Tour of the Trainer
The left menu is the same list of tools you'll find in PlanGrid / Autodesk Build:
**Home, Sheets, Issues & Tasks, Punch List, RFIs, Submittals, Photos, Daily Reports & Time Sheets, Documents & Specs,
Team, Activity**, plus **Training Missions** that check your work automatically.

### Step by step
1. **Settings → Your profile.** Type your name and class year → **Save profile**.
   Your name is stamped on everything you create – just like the real app.
2. **Sheets.** Type `roof` in the search box. Clear it. Use the **discipline** filter to show only
   Waterproofing.
3. Click **R-101 Roof Plan**.
4. **Zoom:** mouse wheel, pinch on a tablet, or the ＋ / － buttons. **Fit:** ⤢ or press `0`.
5. **Pan:** with the ☝ Select tool, click-drag. With any other tool, hold **Space** and drag.
6. Use **‹ R-001** and **R-102 ›** at the top to flip through sheets in order.
7. Find the bubble **2 / R-501** next to drain **RD-2**. With the Select tool, click it – you jump to
   the roof drain detail. Click **1 / R-101** at the bottom right of R-501 to come back.
8. **Documents & Specs → Specifications → 07 54 23 – TPO Roofing.** Find the minimum weld width.
9. Top **search bar**: type `drain` and press **Enter**. Search covers sheets, issues, RFIs,
   documents and photos.

## In PlanGrid / Autodesk Build
- Sheets are organized by **version set** and can be filtered by discipline and tags.
- Detail callouts are **automatically hyperlinked** when the set is published.
- The mobile app downloads sheets for **offline** use – sync before you go up on a roof with no signal.
- There is a **"current" vs "older version"** indicator – look for it every time.

## Field tips
- Check the **revision block** and version date before you set insulation or cut flashings.
- Read the **symbols legend (R-001)** – every architect draws drains, crickets and curbs a little
  differently.
- If the roof plan and the details disagree, don't guess. That's an RFI (Module 5).
