# Module 4 – Issues & Punch Lists

**Time:** 45 min · **App missions:** Module 4 (6 missions) · **Lab:** [Lab 4 – Roof Punch Walk](../labs/lab-04-punch-walk.md)

## Objectives
- Know the difference between an **issue**, a **punch item**, an **RFI** and a markup.
- Pin an issue to an exact location, assign it, give it a due date and attach photos.
- Move an issue through its statuses and comment on it.
- Run and close a roof punch list (like a manufacturer's warranty inspection); export and print it.

## Issue vs punch vs RFI
| | Issue | Punch item | RFI |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | A problem or to-do at a location | Unfinished/defective work near the end | A formal question to the architect |
| Who fixes it | Whoever it's assigned to | Usually the crew that installed it | Architect/consultant answers |
| Roofing example | Other trades left debris on the membrane | Unwelded seam at curb corner | Parapet too low for 8" base flashing |
| Formal? | No | Tracked to closeout / warranty | Yes – numbered, can change cost |

**Issue types in the Trainer:** Issue, Punch, Safety, Quality, **Leak**, Observation, **Warranty**,
Design Coordination.

## Anatomy of a good roofing issue
- **Title:** specific – `Seam probe failed – RTU-1 curb NE outside corner`
- **Location:** pin on the roof plan + roof area + nearest drain/curb/grid
- **Assignee + due date:** someone owns it, by a date
- **Photo:** close-up **and** a step-back shot for context
- **Description:** what's wrong, what "fixed" looks like (e.g. "re-weld, add prefab corner, probe")

Statuses: **Open → In Review → Closed** (or **Void**). Pins: 🔴 open, 🟠 in review, 🟢 closed.
Circles are issues, squares are punch items.

## Typical roofing punch items
- Unwelded / fishmouthed seams (found with a seam probe)
- Missing or loose termination bar, missing sealant
- Base flashing under 8"
- Pipe boot clamp missing or loose
- Drain clamping ring bolts not tightened, strainer missing
- Walkway pads missing to a unit
- Debris, screws or metal shavings left on the membrane
- Scuffs/cuts from other trades (document who did it!)

## Step by step
1. Open **R-101**. Press `I` and click next to **RTU-2**.
2. Title: `Blister in membrane – 3' S of RTU-2`. Type **Quality**. Assign **Maria Lopez**, due Friday.
3. **+ Add photo** (any picture for practice) → **Create**.
4. **Issues** → filter by assignee. Open your issue, add a comment, set **In Review**.
5. **Punch List → + New punch item** three times (use the list above).
6. Tick ✓ on one punch item to close it.
7. **Export CSV** and **Print report** – this is what goes to the manufacturer's rep.

## In PlanGrid / Autodesk Build
- Issues (formerly *Tasks* in PlanGrid) are created with a pin on the sheet or from a list.
- Types and root causes are set by the project admin.
- Assignees get notified; the item shows in their list.
- Final roof inspections are often done on a tablet with the manufacturer's rep – items are
  created live and the report is sent before the warranty is issued.

## Field tips
- Write items so someone who wasn't on the walk can find and fix them.
- Close items only when **verified** (re-probed, re-inspected). Add a "fixed" photo.
- Damage by other trades: photo + issue **the same day**, assigned to the GC.
