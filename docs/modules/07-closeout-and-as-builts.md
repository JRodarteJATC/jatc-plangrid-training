# Module 7 – Closeout, Warranty & As-Builts

**Time:** 30 min · **Lab:** [Lab 7 – As-Builts](../labs/lab-07-as-builts.md) · **Final:** [Final exam](../quizzes/final-exam.md)

## Objectives
- Explain what as-builts and record drawings are and who uses them.
- Keep as-built markups current during the job, not at the end.
- List the roofing and waterproofing closeout and warranty deliverables.

## As-builts
The plans show what was *designed*. As-builts show what was *built*. When a drain moves, a curb is
added, or a penetration shows up that wasn't on the plans, it goes on the as-builts **the same day**.

Who uses them:
- The **owner's maintenance crew** finding a drain under snow or tracing a leak.
- The **roofing contractor** handling a warranty call 12 years from now.
- The **next contractor** adding equipment or reroofing.

### As-built rules of thumb
- Use the **As-Built layer** (dashed in the Trainer) and a consistent color.
- Dimension from fixed references (parapet walls, gridlines) – especially buried items like
  below-grade waterproofing terminations and footing drain cleanouts.
- Show every added penetration, curb and sleeper.
- Reference the RFI/ASI that authorized a change: `PER RFI-002`.
- Stamp sheets **AS-BUILT** when complete.

## Roofing & waterproofing closeout package
- As-built roof plan (PDF export with markups)
- Manufacturer's final inspection report + **NDL warranty**
- Contractor's workmanship warranty (e.g. 2 years)
- Closed punch list with photos
- Product data / approved submittals
- Maintenance instructions (walk-pad use, drain cleaning, annual inspection)
- Below-grade WP: inspection photos before backfill, material warranty
- Elevator pit: flood-test record
- Attic stock (extra membrane, pads, sealant) transmittal

## Exporting from the Trainer
- **⤓ PNG** on a sheet – exports the sheet with visible markups.
- **Punch List → Print report** – items with photos.
- **Settings → Export backup** – the whole project.
