# Module 6 – Photos, Daily Reports & Revisions

**Time:** 60 min · **App missions:** Module 6 (7 missions) · **Lab:** [Lab 6 – A Day on the Roof](../labs/lab-06-field-day.md)

## Objectives
- Take useful jobsite photos and pin them to a location.
- Fill out a daily report, toolbox talk, pre-task plan and inspection request.
- Understand revisions and ASIs; publish a new sheet version.
- Use **compare / overlay** to find what changed.

## Photos
Photos protect the roofing contractor and the warranty. Take them:
- **Before cover-up** – deck before insulation, insulation/taper before cover board, below-grade
  waterproofing **before backfill**, drain sumps before membrane.
- **Of problems** – deck damage, wet insulation, other trades' damage – attached to an issue.
- **Of completed details** – corners, boots, drains, curbs, terminations.
- **Weather conditions** – standing water, frost, wind – when you can't work.

Good photos have **context** (step back) and **detail** (step in). Caption them:
`RD-2 clamping ring torqued, strainer set – 9/25` beats `IMG_4432`.

In the Trainer: open a sheet → 📷 tool (`O`) → click the location → choose photos.

## Daily reports & forms
The daily report is the **legal diary** of the job. In roofing it's also how you prove you weren't
installing roof in the rain.

| Section | What to write |
|---|---|
| Weather | Conditions, temps, **wind**, rain, dew/frost start time |
| Manpower | Classification, company, headcount, hours |
| Work performed | Specific: *"Area A: VR + 2 layers flat iso, 0–30'. Tapered at RD-1. Night seal at 30'."* |
| Delays | Rain, dew, wind, waiting on curbs/mech, deck not ready – with times |
| Safety | Toolbox talk topic, warning lines, heat, near-misses |
| Equipment / visitors | Hoist, welders, lift; mfr rep and inspector visits |

**Toolbox Talk** – topic, key points, sign-in (see *Documents → Safety → Fall Protection*).
**Pre-Task Plan / JHA** – steps → hazards → controls, signed by the crew.
**Inspection request** – deck inspection, tapered layout, mid-roof mfr inspection, below-grade WP
before backfill, **elevator pit flood test**, final/warranty inspection.

## Revisions
Drawings change after bid through **ASIs** (small changes), **bulletins / change orders**, and **RFI
answers**. Revised sheets show a **revision cloud** and a **delta (△1)**, and the title block's
revision list is updated.

### Compare / overlay
Open **R-101 → ⇄ Compare → Rev 0.** Colors:
- **Blue** – added in the newer revision
- **Red** – removed
- **Gray** – unchanged

Find everything **ASI-01** added, then read **Documents → ASIs & Bulletins → ASI-01** and make
sure you found it all. Ask: *did the ASI revise every sheet it affects?* (Hint: check R-102.)

## In PlanGrid / Autodesk Build
Version sets, automatic sheet matching, carry-forward of markups/issues, and a **compare** overlay
are core features. The app warns you when you're looking at an old version.
